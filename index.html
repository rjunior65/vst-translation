Assistance

Work Permit Assistance

We assist with documentation and preparation for Work Permit applications.

VST provides assistance with the documentation and preparation involved in Work Permit applications. We help you organize, translate and prepare the paperwork you may need.

How VST Assists

  • Reviewing and organizing your documents
  • Translating documents when required
  • Helping you understand the general process
  • Preparing your application paperwork

Documents That May Be Necessary

  • Identification and personal documents
  • Certificates (birth, marriage, etc.)
  • Professional or educational documents
  • Other supporting documentation

Important Information

VST is a private services company. We are not a government agency and we do not guarantee approval of any application. We provide professional assistance with documentation and application preparation.
